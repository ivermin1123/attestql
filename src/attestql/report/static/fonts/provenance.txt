The fonts on these pages, where they came from, and under what licence.

Three Latin subsets of IBM Plex, self-hosted so that a rendered report needs nothing from a
network and states nothing to one. The licence is beside them in OFL.txt, whole and
unaltered, which is what the SIL Open Font License 1.1 asks of anyone who redistributes the
files. NOTICE names them too.

The files are the packages' own pre-split Latin subsets, copied byte for byte and not
resubset here. The subset covers U+0000, U+000D, U+0020-007E, U+00A0-00FF, U+0131,
U+0152-0153, U+02C6, U+02DA, U+02DC, U+2013-2014, U+2018-201A, U+201C-201E, U+2020-2022,
U+2026, U+2030, U+2039-203A, U+2044, U+20AC, U+2122, U+2212, U+FB01-FB02: every character
these pages write except the two triangles that mark the gold and the second side, which
the reader's own system font draws.
